CG200 Engineering & Procurement FAQ

What is the exact displacement, power, and torque of the CHIXIANG CG200?

The CG200 (CX163FML) has an actual displacement of 197.0 cc with a 63.5 × 62.2 mm bore and stroke. Rated max power is 10 kW @ 7,500 rpm and max torque is 13.5 N·m @ 6,500 rpm, with an 8.5–9.3 : 1 compression ratio.

What is the difference between air-cooled CG200 and water-cooled CG200SB?

Both share a 63.5 × 62.2 mm bore and stroke (197.0 cc), but the CG200 uses natural air cooling (31.3 kg, 380 × 355 × 500 mm) for lower complexity and lighter commuter or utility builds, whereas the CG200SB uses liquid cooling (425 × 385 × 530 mm, 10.5 kW / 15.0 N·m) for sustained heavy-load cargo duty.

Can CG200 engines be ordered with a balance shaft or reverse gearbox?

Yes. Standard CG200 ships with a 5-speed constant-mesh forward transmission. Balance-shaft configurations and model-matched reverse gearboxes can be specified per order BOM before Proforma Invoice confirmation.

What is the minimum order quantity and standard export packaging for CG200?

Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines, and you can mix CG200 with CG150, CB, horizontal, or water-cooled models plus spare parts in one container. Regular production lead time is approximately 10 working days under FOB Guangzhou terms (other major Chinese ports by arrangement).

Are engine mounting dimensions and warranty terms provided?

Yes. Factory installation dimension drawings are supplied with your quotation so your engineering team can verify frame lugs, output shaft spline, and wiring plugs. Every engine undergoes 100% pre-shipment hot and cold bench testing and carries a 60-day warranty after destination port arrival for verified manufacturing or assembly defects.
