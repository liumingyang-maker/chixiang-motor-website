CG water-cooled engines: before you request a quotation

Engineered for heavy-duty cargo tricycles and commercial motorcycles, matched to your vehicle payload, cooling setup, and mounting interface. Built-in or external reverse-gear configurations are available across designated CG water-cooled models; specify your drive setup when requesting a quote.

Specify your target displacement, mounting lug spacing, output-shaft spline count, and cooling setup so we can match matching service parts and export packing in one quotation.

Approved family configurations

CG balance-shaft configuration

For motorcycle applications, a balance shaft helps reduce single-cylinder vibration and improve running smoothness, with international 5-speed, rotary, or built-in reverse gearboxes tailored to the platform.

Tsunami water-cooled family

A strengthened CG water-cooled family featuring dual cooling fins and enlarged sump oil capacity for sustained heavy-duty thermal stability in cargo-tricycle and utility platforms.

HW Water heavy-duty family

A heavy-duty CG water-cooled upgrade with cooling fins, 1.5 L oil capacity, heavy-duty internal parts, a 20-roller clutch and a high-output magneto. Standard 5-speed forward transmission (compatible with external reverse gearboxes).

Automatic-clutch water-cooled family

Available only in CG150 and CG175 classes, with automatic centrifugal clutch. It is a separate family from built-in-reverse configurations.

FAQ

How do you verify frame & driveline fitment?

Provide your target model and vehicle setup to verify four core engineering interfaces: mounting lug spacing, output-shaft spline teeth, magneto pole count, and international vs. rotary shift pattern.

What OEM/ODM configurations are available?

Custom crankcase branding, export carton packaging, starting method, clutch type, and gear pattern (international 5-speed, rotary, or reverse options) are supported from 50-unit wholesale orders.

Can matching spare parts ship with engines?

Yes. Cylinder kits, heads, clutches, crankcases, and electrical parts ship consolidated in the same mixed container as complete engines.
