CG250SB Engineering & Procurement FAQ

How is CG250SB (246.3 cc) different from air-cooled CG250 (229.5 cc)?

CG250SB is a 70 × 64 mm water-cooled engine (246.3 cc actual, 11.5 kW, 19.5 N·m, 425 × 385 × 530 mm), whereas air-cooled CG250 uses a 67 × 65 mm bore and stroke (229.5 cc actual, 10.5 kW, 16.5 N·m, 380 × 355 × 500 mm). Always specify CG250SB by exact model code when ordering.

When should a buyer choose CG250SB vs HW250 or HW300?

Choose CG250SB (32.9 kg gross package) for mainstream cargo-tricycle builds prioritizing lighter dry weight and lower unit cost; choose HW250 or HW300 for sustained extreme payloads requiring a 1.5 L oil sump, 20-roller clutch, and ~80% HW250/HW300 spare-parts commonality.

Is reverse gear standard on the CG250SB?

Reverse is not automatically included across all CG water-cooled models. Built-in or external reverse-gear configurations are available across designated CG water-cooled models and must be specified in your order BOM.

What is the export carton size and package weight of CG250SB?

CG250SB ships in a 425 × 385 × 530 mm export carton with 32.9 kg gross package weight, or on 12-unit export pallets for mixed-container loading.

What are the MOQ, lead time, and warranty terms for CG250SB?

Standard MOQ is 50 complete engines (mixed models and water-cooled service kits supported), regular lead time is ~10 working days under FOB Guangzhou terms, and every unit includes 100% pre-shipment hot/cold bench testing plus a 60-day post-arrival warranty.
