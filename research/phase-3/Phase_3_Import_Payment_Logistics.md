# Import, Payment and Logistics Validation

No country has a Phase 3 PASS for import/payment/logistics. HS classification, engine-number/registration treatment, duty/tax, destination handling, usable payment route, freight route and compliance must be validated with a qualified importer, broker, freight forwarder, and where relevant legal/compliance adviser.

Russia is `FAIL` at this gate until sanctions, bank/payment, freight, customs and advertising-platform compliance are independently cleared. No conclusion is drawn from brand or marketplace visibility.

Factory terms may be discussed only as existing confirmed capability (EXW/FOB, T/T, USD/CNY and delivery to a buyer's China forwarder); no price, freight, delivery-time or tax figure is assumed.
