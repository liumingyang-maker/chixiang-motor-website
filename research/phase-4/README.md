## Scope

Phase 4 researches six fixed countries in order: Peru, Uzbekistan, Ecuador, Tanzania, Russia and Colombia. It produces product-demand rankings, country/product scoring, competitor-product mapping, keyword and negative-keyword research, campaign architecture, landing-page requirements and budget allocation ratios. It does not modify production pages or advertising accounts.

## Evidence rules

Every Phase 4 claim uses a distinct `P4-` Evidence ID in `data/phase4_evidence_log.csv`. Phase 3 IDs may be cited only as baseline references. Where reliable keyword-tool data is unavailable, demand and competition use `High`, `Medium`, `Low` or `Unknown` and are labelled `PROXY DATA`; exact search volume, CPC, price, profit, market share and compatibility are prohibited unless sourced.

## Safety boundary

No ads launched. No buyers contacted. No production inquiries submitted.
