Motorcycle & Cargo-Tricycle Engine Manufacturer in Chongqing, China

CHIXIANG MOTOR manufactures and exports CG125–CG250, CB150–CB250, horizontal, and water-cooled engines for international motorcycle distributors, cargo-tricycle importers, and vehicle assemblers. Operating from Hangu Town, Chongqing, we also supply aftermarket replacement engines and spare parts for Bajaj Boxer, Bajaj RE, TVS HLX, and TVS King platforms—supporting mixed-model wholesale orders starting from 50 units.

Engine Supply for Global Importers, Distributors & Assemblers

Direct Chongqing manufacturing capacity (10,000+ units/month) engineered for B2B distributors, assemblers, and fleet importers.

CG, CB & Horizontal Engine Wholesale

Factory-direct CG air-cooled (125–250 cc), CB (150–250 cc), Horizontal CX (110–150 cc), and AC320 ATV engines built for commercial motorcycles, underbones, and assembly lines.

Cargo-Tricycle Engine Solutions

CG Water-Cooled (CG150SB–CG250SB), Tsunami dual-cooling, and HW Heavy-Duty (HW200–HW350, 1.5 L oil, 20-roller clutch, 18-stage DC magneto) powertrains built for sustained heavy cargo loads.

Bajaj & TVS Aftermarket Supply

OEM-equivalent replacement engines, crankcases, covers, and overhaul kits for Bajaj Boxer CT100/BM150, Bajaj RE, TVS HLX 125/150, and TVS King platforms.

Engine Configuration & Technical Support

Direct engineering alignment on mounting lug spacing, output-shaft spline teeth, starting method, transmission pattern, and air vs. water cooling configuration.

Wholesale Orders & Export Services

Standard complete-engine MOQ starts at 50 units, with mixed-model orders supported. Typical lead time for standard configurations is approximately 10 working days after order confirmation. FOB Guangzhou is the standard export term, with other major Chinese ports available by arrangement, backed by a 60-day warranty after destination port arrival.
