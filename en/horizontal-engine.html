FAQ

How do you verify frame & driveline fitment?

Provide your target model and vehicle setup to verify four core engineering interfaces: mounting lug spacing, output-shaft spline teeth, magneto pole count, and international vs. rotary shift pattern.

What OEM/ODM configurations are available?

Custom crankcase branding, export carton packaging, starting method, clutch type, and gear pattern (international 5-speed, rotary, or reverse options) are supported from 50-unit wholesale orders.

Can matching spare parts ship with engines?

Yes. Cylinder kits, heads, clutches, crankcases, and electrical parts ship consolidated in the same mixed container as complete engines.

Tell us your engine model, quantity and destination market.
