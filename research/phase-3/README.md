# Chixiang Motor — Phase 3 Market Validation

## Scope

This directory is a research-only Phase 3 validation package. It does not alter production pages, submit forms, contact buyers, create campaigns, or launch ads.

Phase 2B is an immutable 2026-07-15 evidence snapshot. Its rankings are inputs, not outputs of this work.

## Lanes

- Lane A: Peru, Ecuador, Tanzania — full public-evidence validation.
- Lane B: Uzbekistan, Russia — accelerated validation; factory business signals are labelled inputs, not independent market proof.
- Lane C: Colombia — limited validation of Chinese CG complete-engine demand only.

## Status vocabulary

`PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT TESTED`, `INPUT REQUIRED`, and `MANUAL TEST REQUIRED` have their literal meanings. `NOT TESTED` is never treated as `PASS`.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Same displacement does not prove compatibility.
- Parts listings do not prove complete-engine demand.
- Indian Bajaj, TVS, Hero, Piaggio Ape, Mahindra, and Royal Enfield platforms are excluded from serviceable replacement-engine opportunity.
- Factory prices, freight, duties, selling prices, MOQ, lead time, warranty, certification, stock, and search volume/CPC are never invented.
- Ads Launch remains `NOT APPROVED` for every country in Phase 3.

## Evidence records

All public claims used in a workbook or gate must appear in `data/evidence_register.csv`. Source notes are grouped by country under `sources/` and country folders.
