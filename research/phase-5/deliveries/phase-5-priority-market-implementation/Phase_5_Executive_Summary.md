# Phase 5 Executive Summary

Phase 5 converts the frozen Phase 4 research into a reviewable website and advertising-design package without changing any Phase 4 gate.

## Implemented market direction

- **Peru:** CG200 air-cooled first, CG150 air-cooled second, with a separate aftermarket/spares offer. The page targets distributors, assemblers and work-motorcycle demand while requiring technical qualification.
- **Uzbekistan:** 150–250 cc water-cooled cargo direction, with spares for a confirmed engine configuration. The new canonical page separates market direction from compatibility proof.
- **Russia:** lower horizontal 140 cc first; Honda-style horizontal 140 second; CB/pit-bike 140–150 only as research/qualification. Platform names are not fit claims.
- **Colombia:** SEO and distributor development first. Complete-engine demand is not verified from available public evidence; paid complete-engine search is not recommended.

## Design-only advertising assets

Campaign structures, ad copy, localized negatives, keyword-tool inputs and three USD 1,000 allocation scenarios are prepared as drafts. Search volume, CPC, profit, price, MOQ and market share were not invented. Tool metrics remain blank or `INPUT REQUIRED`.

## Gate
