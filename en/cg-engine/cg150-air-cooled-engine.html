CG150 Engineering & Procurement FAQ

What are the verified displacement, power, and weight of the CHIXIANG CG150?

The CG150 (CX162FMJ) has an actual displacement of 149 cc (62 × 49.5 mm bore and stroke), delivering 8.5 kW @ 8,000 rpm and 10 N·m @ 7,500 rpm with a 9.2 : 1 compression ratio and 31.05 kg net weight.

Can I order the CG150 with a PZ26 or PZ27 carburetor and high-output magneto?

Yes. Carburetor specification (PZ26 or PZ27) and high-output magneto stator windings are confirmed in the order BOM and Proforma Invoice prior to assembly.

Is a balance-shaft version available for the CG150?

Yes. Standard CG150 uses the non-balance pushrod architecture, and a CG balance-shaft configuration can be specified for commuter motorcycle applications requiring lower vibration.

How does CG150 compare to CB150 for commercial fleet assembly?

CG150 uses an OHV pushrod valve train (8.5 kW, 31.05 kg) prized for straightforward field maintenance and low-RPM work durability, whereas CB150 uses a chain-driven OHC layout (8.2 kW, 29 kg) for higher-revving street agility.

What is the MOQ and mixed-loading policy for CG150?

Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines, supporting mixed loading with CG200, CB, horizontal, or water-cooled engines plus overhaul kits under FOB Guangzhou terms (~10 working days regular lead time, 60-day post-arrival warranty).
