HW250 & HW300 vs CG250 Water-Cooled Cargo Tricycle Engines: Selection & 80% Parts Sharing

Published: November 2026 · Engineering & Procurement Guide by CHIXIANG MOTOR

Quick Selection Verdict

Choose CG250SB (246.3 cc, 32.9 kg package) for standard cargo-tricycle builds prioritizing lighter dry weight and lower unit cost; choose HW250 (249 ml, 38.1 kg net) or HW300 (300 ml, 12.5 kW / 21 N·m, 39 kg net) for sustained heavy payloads, steep gradients, 1.5 L oil capacity, 20-roller clutch durability, and ~80% cross-displacement spare parts sharing across 250cc and 300cc fleets.

Cargo-tricycle assemblers and regional distributors often assume every 250cc water-cooled vertical engine built around a 70 × 64 mm bore and stroke performs identically in the field. On a dynamometer at light load, peak output looks close—but under sustained heavy-load cargo duty in hot climates, crankcase oil volume, one-way clutch strength, magneto output, and spare-parts commonality determine warranty cost and distributor cash flow.

2. Why HW250 Weighs 5.2 kg More Than CG250SB—and Where That Metal Goes

When comparing HW250 (38.1 kg net) against CG250SB (32.9 kg), the extra mass is not cosmetic. It reflects four structural upgrades specifically engineered for heavy cargo tricycles operating at high load and low vehicle speed:

  • Dual thermal buffer (water jacket + cylinder cooling fins + 1.5 L sump): Enlarged crankcase volume holds 1.5 L of engine oil across the HW series, slowing oil thermal breakdown when radiator airflow drops on steep climbs or traffic-jammed urban freight routes.
  • 20-roller one-way starter clutch: High-compression 250cc–300cc water-cooled singles punish conventional starter clutches during hot restarts. The 20-roller assembly spreads engagement load across more contact points to reduce starter-clutch slippage.
  • High-output DC magneto & PZ30 fueling: Supports auxiliary cooling-fan draw, high-wattage tricycle lighting, and steady idle/midrange fueling under heavy throttle.
  • External reverse gearbox driveline isolation: Instead of squeezing a reverse gear cluster inside the main crankcase, HW200–HW350 keep a full heavy-duty 5-speed forward gearset inside the crankcase and pair with an external tricycle reverse unit—simplifying field service when reverse gears wear.

4. Decision Checklist: Which Model Should You Specify?

  • Pick CG250SB when: Your market demands lower unit landed price on flat or moderate routes, lighter frame load (32.9 kg), or an order configuration built around CG-standard reverse options.
  • Pick HW250 when: You want 250cc displacement regulation compliance combined with HW heavy-duty durability (1.5 L oil, 20-roller clutch, external reverse box) and shared spares compatibility with HW300 units.
  • Pick HW300 when: Vehicles haul maximum agricultural, construction, or steep-hill payloads where lower-rpm peak torque (21 N·m @ 4,500 rpm vs 19.5 N·m @ 5,000 rpm) reduces clutch slip and gear-hunting on inclines.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between CHIXIANG HW250 and standard CG250SB water-cooled engines?

Both use a 70 × 64 mm bore and stroke producing 11.5 kW @ 7,000 rpm and 19.5 N·m @ 5,000 rpm, but HW250 is built on the heavy-duty HW Water architecture (38.1 kg net weight vs 32.9 kg package weight on CG250SB) with cylinder cooling fins, 1.5 L oil capacity, a 20-roller one-way clutch, high-output magneto, PZ30 carburetor specification, and a 5-speed forward transmission matched to external reverse gearboxes.

How much parts commonality exists between HW250 and HW300?

Approximately 80% of core components are interchangeable across CHIXIANG HW250 and HW300 platforms—including left and right crankcases, 20-roller clutch assemblies, 5-speed transmission shafts, water pumps, high-output magnetos, starter motors, and full fastener kits—while cylinder blocks, pistons, and crankshaft connecting-rod assemblies are matched by displacement.

Does the HW250 or HW300 have a built-in reverse gear?

No. Across the HW Water series (HW200, HW250, HW300, HW350), CHIXIANG MOTOR supplies a standard 5-speed forward transmission engineered to pair with external cargo-tricycle reverse gearboxes. Standard CG water-cooled models such as CG250SB should be confirmed per model and order when reverse configuration is specified.

Can importers mix CG250SB, HW250, HW300 engines and factory-matched service kits in one order?

Yes. Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines with mixed-model loading supported across CG, CB, horizontal, and HW water-cooled families, alongside consolidated HW service kits and overhaul spares. Standard production lead time is approximately 10 working days on FOB Guangzhou terms.

How are HW and CG water-cooled engines tested before export?

Every unit undergoes 100% pre-shipment hot and cold bench testing on the assembly line, backed by two factory testing laboratories equipped with eddy-current dynamometers, exhaust gas analyzers, and incoming metallurgical and hardness inspection. Verified manufacturing or assembly defects within the 60-day post-arrival warranty are supported with free replacement parts in the next container.
