2. Five-Point Pre-Order Engineering Interface Checklist

Before finalizing a production bill of materials, wholesale buyers and assembly engineers should verify five mechanical and electrical interfaces to prevent frame-level installation mismatches:

  1. Engine Prefix & Casting Reference Code Verification
    Record the exact stamped crankcase prefix and OEM part numbers from the target fleet (e.g., DU10, DU101218, 36DU4079, or 52PF2129 for Boxer CT100/BM100; 36PF0004 through 36PF0008 or JA541212 for BM150). Confirm bore-and-stroke variant before casting allocation—specifically distinguishing standard 99.27 cc vs. 102 cc builds on CT100 orders, and 124.53 cc vs. 147.49 cc builds on TVS HLX orders.
  2. Compression Ratio & Fuel Calibration (Petrol vs. CNG/LPG)
    Three-wheeler engines—specifically the 198.88 cc Bajaj RE 4S / RE Compact 200 and 199.26 cc TVS King Deluxe / GS 200—operate in markets split between gasoline and gaseous fuels. Specify petrol versus CNG/LPG fuel operation prior to order confirmation so cylinder head combustion chamber volume, valve seat metallurgy, and ignition timing curves are matched to the target fuel.
  3. Output Sprocket, Flange Spline & Final Drive Interface
    For two-wheeler commuter platforms, verify front drive sprocket pitch and tooth count (such as the 428-14T sprocket standard on the Bajaj Boxer CT100/BM100) as well as shift-shaft spline geometry on 4-speed versus 5-speed TVS HLX variants. For rear-mounted three-wheeler powertrains (Bajaj RE and TVS King), confirm differential/drive flange spline count and coupling bolt circle diameter.
  4. Wiring Harness Pins, Ignition & Magneto Connector Matching
    Confirm CDI/ECU stator trigger pinouts, gear-position indicator switch terminals, and starting architecture (kick-start only vs. electric-start equipped) against the local vehicle wiring harness. Verify charging coil output specification and regulator-rectifier plug housing per model year for high-output magneto configurations.
  5. Crankcase Reverse Chamber & Lubrication Gallery Architecture
    Inspect integrated 4F+1R reverse gear chamber dimensions, shift-cable/linkage bracket bosses, and breather routing on Bajaj RE 4S and TVS King 200 crankcases. Confirm oil sump fill volume (1.0 L on CT100/BM100) and fine oil filter gallery interfaces on TVS King Duralife-compatible castings to maintain oil pressure under high ambient temperatures.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are CHIXIANG Bajaj and TVS replacement engines OEM-branded factory units?

No. CHIXIANG MOTOR manufactures independent aftermarket replacement engines, crankcases, and service parts engineered to match the mounting dimensions, bore-and-stroke geometry, and OEM reference interfaces of Bajaj Boxer, Bajaj RE, TVS HLX, and TVS King models. OEM part numbers are cited strictly for technical fitment cross-referencing.

2. Can we mix Indian replacement engines with CG or water-cooled tricycle engines in one container?

Yes. Our standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines, and buyers routinely combine Bajaj/TVS replacement engines and crankcase castings with CG, CB, horizontal, or water-cooled cargo-tricycle engines and matching spare parts in a single mixed container.

3. What information do we need to verify before ordering Bajaj RE 4S or TVS King 200 three-wheeler engines?

Buyers should verify fuel type (petrol vs. CNG/LPG compression specification), 4F+1R reverse-chamber shift linkage orientation, output drive flange spline count, and wiring harness connector pinouts prior to production scheduling.

4. How does CHIXIANG verify engine quality before export shipment?

Every complete engine passes 100% pre-shipment hot and cold bench testing to verify compression, shifting smoothness, reverse-gear engagement, magneto output, and oil-tightness, supported by our dual in-house dynamometer and metallurgical laboratories in Hangu, Chongqing.

5. What is your warranty and spare-part claim process for international distributors?

CHIXIANG provides a 60-day warranty after destination port arrival. If a manufacturing defect occurs within the warranty window, buyers submit the engine serial number along with clear photo or video verification, and free replacement parts are dispatched in the next container shipment.
