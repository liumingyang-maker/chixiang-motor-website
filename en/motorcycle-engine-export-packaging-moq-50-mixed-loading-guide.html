Container Loading Density: Why Fixed 20GP / 40HQ Counts Are Calculated Per BOM

Single-unit volumes above are derived strictly from external carton dimensions for preliminary freight estimation. CHIXIANG does not quote a single fixed unit count for 20GP or 40HQ containers because actual loading density varies across four operational variables:

  • Floor-Loaded Cartons vs. 12-Unit Export Pallets: Palletizing engines in 12-unit blocks speeds up forklift unloading at destination warehouses but reduces usable vertical and floor clearance compared with loose carton stacking (pallet base dimensions and tare weight are specified per shipment).
  • Gross Payload Limits on Heavy Water-Cooled Units: Because HW200–HW350 engines weigh 38–40.4 kg net per unit, shipments weighted heavily toward HW series often reach maximum container payload or destination highway axle-weight limits before filling internal cubic volume.
  • Mixed-Model Stacking Profiles: Combining low-profile horizontal engines (235–280 mm height) with tall vertical CG/HW cartons (500–530 mm height) requires tier planning to protect lower cartons from crushing.
  • Consolidated Spare Parts Allocation: Adding boxed cylinder kits, crankcases, and service parts alters residual cube space. Exact container loading plans are calculated against the final Order BOM and destination port weight regulations before booking vessel space.

Operating from Hangu Town, Jiulongpo District, Chongqing with industry experience since 2003 (registered 2007) and a monthly production capacity of 10,000+ units, CHIXIANG structures wholesale exports to help regional distributors balance inventory turnover without over-committing to a single displacement.

  1. Anchor the Core Engine Mix (50+ Complete Units Total): Combine fast-moving commuter engines (CG air-cooled, CB OHC, or Horizontal CX series) with higher-margin cargo-tricycle units (CG water-cooled or HW200–HW350 heavy-duty series) to meet the 50-unit standard complete-engine MOQ.
  2. Consolidate High-Turnover Service Kits & Spares: Allocate remaining container weight or cubic space to factory-matched replacement assemblies—such as HW water-cooled cylinder kits, 20-roller clutches, crankshafts, gasket/seal overhaul kits, or Bajaj/TVS aftermarket crankcases and service components listed in our Engine Spare Parts Directory.
  3. Separate Palletized & Cartonized Cargo Clearly: Pack heavy complete engines on 12-unit export pallets for rapid warehouse receiving while consolidating smaller spare-part cartons into clearly numbered master boxes matched item-for-item to the packing list.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I mix different engine series and spare parts to meet the 50-unit MOQ?

Yes. The standard MOQ is 50 complete engines, and distributors regularly combine CG air-cooled, CG water-cooled, HW heavy-duty water-cooled, CB, and horizontal models within the same 50-unit+ shipment, along with consolidated HW or Bajaj/TVS replacement parts and service kits.

2. Why doesn't CHIXIANG list a single fixed unit count for a 20GP or 40HQ container?

Actual container loading density changes depending on whether engines ship in loose cartons or on 12-unit export pallets, the ratio of compact horizontal engines (235–280 mm height) to heavy HW water-cooled engines (38–40.4 kg net), spare-parts volume, and destination port weight restrictions. We calculate exact loading plans against your finalized Order BOM.

3. Are carburetors (PZ26/PZ27/PZ30) and CDI kits automatically packed with every complete engine?

No. Complete engines ship standard with the core engine assembly and factory stator/rotor coils. Kick-starter levers, gearshift levers, intake manifolds, carburetors (PZ26/PZ27/PZ30), and CDI/regulator/relay kits are optional items explicitly specified on the Order BOM and Proforma Invoice.

4. Should wholesale importers choose individual cartons or 12-unit export pallets?

Individual cartons maximize volumetric utilization inside mixed containers and simplify manual unloading where forklifts are unavailable. Twelve-unit export pallets accelerate port and warehouse handling for larger repeat orders. Both packing options are specified per Order BOM prior to production.

5. What are the standard lead time, export port, and warranty claim steps?

Regular configurations require approximately 10 working days after order confirmation under default FOB Guangzhou terms (other major Chinese ports by arrangement). Under our 60-day warranty after destination port arrival, buyers submit the engine serial number along with photo or video verification, and free replacement parts are dispatched in the next container shipment.
