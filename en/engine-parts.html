FAQ

How do you match replacement parts by code?

Match via engine code, Chixiang PN / OEM reference, or sample measurement across bore × stroke, wristpin diameter, and magneto connector pin count.

How is parts compatibility verified?

Parts are verified directly against engine platform specs, cylinder bore × stroke, spline tooth count, and stator pole configuration.

Can parts ship with engine orders?

Yes. Overhaul kits, wear parts, and engine assemblies consolidate directly in 50-unit+ mixed wholesale containers.
