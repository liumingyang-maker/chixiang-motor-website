# Batch 1 Execution — Peru and Uzbekistan

## Objective

Produce Phase 4 research outputs for Peru and Uzbekistan only. Stop after Batch 1; do not research Ecuador, Tanzania, Russia or Colombia in this batch.

## Required output records

For each country, create at least ten ranked product directions, with a score, opportunity tier, evidence IDs, rationale, confidence, demand type, primary vehicle/application and an explicit distinction between complete-engine, new assembly, replacement and parts demand. No same-displacement compatibility inference is permitted.

Build complete keyword and negative-keyword research, competitor-product mapping, Google/Yandex campaign/ad-group architecture, landing-page requirements and USD 1,000 allocation *percentages*. When no reliable platform data exists, label all proxy fields `PROXY DATA` and leave numeric volume/CPC/profit fields blank.

## Fixed constraints

- Peru: Google Search architecture only; research local Spanish terms including work motorcycle, motocarro and cargo-tricycle terms.
- Uzbekistan: assess Google and Yandex separately; research Russian and Uzbek terminology; do not infer engine fit from a vehicle category or competitor brand.
- Do not write or alter advertising accounts, production pages, Phase 3 Gates or Ads Launch status.
- Do not contact buyers or submit any form.

## Evidence acceptance

Use official manufacturer/importer/industry/marketplace/visible-SERP sources with date and limitation. P4 evidence IDs must be unique and must not reuse P3 IDs. Parts-only evidence cannot be scored as complete-engine demand. Indian-platform terms may be excluded only at the applicable campaign/ad-group level, never by erasing valid Chinese-platform research.

## QA stop condition

Batch 1 can be delivered only after QA confirms unique P4 evidence IDs, cited score records, no invented exact volume/CPC/price/profit, Phase 3 gate invariance, Ads Launch `NOT APPROVED`, and no outreach/production changes.
