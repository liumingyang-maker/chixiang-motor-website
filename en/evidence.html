Engineering & Quality Evidence Center

Verifiable manufacturing capacity, laboratory dynamometer testing, core component material & hardness standards, container loading protocols, and public records of Chongqing Chixiang Motorcycle Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

Manufacturing & Engineering Verification

Key operational, laboratory, and export-delivery standards executed at our Chongqing facility.

10,000+ Units Monthly Production Capacity

Standardized assembly lines in Hangu Town, Chongqing support scalable batch production across CG, CB, horizontal, water-cooled, and ATV engine platforms—scaling to 10,000+ engines per month to meet distributor and OEM schedules.

Laboratory Dynamometer & Emission Verification

Two dedicated engine testing laboratories equipped with eddy-current dynamometers and exhaust gas analyzers verify torque output, power curves, thermal stability, oil pressure, and emission compliance across production batches.

Core Component Material & Hardness Standards

Incoming metallurgical and dimensional inspections verify alloy grade, heat-treatment depth, and Rockwell/Vickers surface hardness on critical wear parts—including crankshafts, transmission gears, cylinder blocks, camshafts, and clutch assemblies—before line release.

Container Loading, FOB Terms & 60-Day Arrival Warranty

Finished engines are drained/sealed per export protocol, packed in reinforced cartons or fumigation-free wooden crates/pallets with moisture-barrier protection, barcoded by engine serial and batch number, and braced for ocean container transit. Standard MOQ is 50 units (mixed models supported), typical dispatch lead time is ~10 working days (default FOB Guangzhou; other major Chinese ports supported), backed by a 60-day warranty after destination port arrival for verified manufacturing/assembly defects with replacement parts shipped free with the next container.
