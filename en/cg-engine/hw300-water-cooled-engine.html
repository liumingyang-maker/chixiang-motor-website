HW300 Engineering & Procurement FAQ

What is the actual displacement and torque of the CHIXIANG HW300?

The HW300 (300cc Class) has a 74 × 64 mm bore and stroke, yielding an actual geometric displacement of 275.3 mL. It delivers 12.5 kW @ 6,500 rpm and 21 N·m @ 4,500 rpm with a 10.4 : 1 compression ratio.

How does the HW300 differ from standard water-cooled CG250SB engines?

HW300 is built on the heavy-duty HW Water architecture with auxiliary cylinder cooling fins, a 1.5 L crankcase oil capacity, a 20-roller one-way clutch, and a high-output DC magneto—designed for sustained heavy cargo loads and steep gradients.

Which spare parts are shared between HW250 and HW300?

Approximately 80% of core assemblies—including left/right crankcases, 20-roller clutches, 5-speed transmission shafts, water pumps, high-output DC magnetos, and fastener kits—are shared across HW250 and HW300, reducing distributor spare-parts inventory capital.

Does the HW300 include a built-in reverse gear?

No. The HW300 uses a standard 5-speed forward transmission without built-in reverse and is engineered to pair with external cargo-tricycle reverse gearboxes.

Can I consolidate HW300 complete engines with HW service kits in one container?

Yes. Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines (mixed HW200–HW350 and CG/CB models supported), and factory-matched HW service kits or core assemblies can be loaded in the same container under FOB Guangzhou terms (~10 working days regular lead time, 60-day post-arrival warranty).
