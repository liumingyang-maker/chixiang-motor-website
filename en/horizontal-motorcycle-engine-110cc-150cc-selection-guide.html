110cc–150cc Horizontal Motorcycle Engines (CX152FMH, CX1P56FMJ & CX1P60FMJ): YX Reference Alignment, Clutch Types & Internal Oil Circuit

Published: November 2026 · Engineering & Procurement Guide by CHIXIANG MOTOR

Quick Answer Verdict

Chongqing Chixiang Motorcycle Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (CHIXIANG MOTOR) manufactures five 4-speed horizontal motorcycle engines spanning 106.7 ml to 149.8 ml with automatic wet, semi-auto, and manual clutches. Choose CX152FMH variants for 110cc–130-series underbones and ATVs, CX1P56FMJ for YX140 market-reference alignment, and CX1P60FMJ (W150-2 reference) for high-revving 150cc builds with an internal cylinder-head oil circuit.

Market-Reference & Displacement Clarity Note: References to YX140 and W150-2 are used strictly as market-reference alignment to help importers identify chassis class and performance tier—not as third-party brand claims. Commercial catalog labels such as 125 cc class (CX152FMH-6, actual 107 ml) and (130) (CX152FMH-5B (130), actual 123.67 ml) are always paired with verified measured displacement below to prevent customs or engineering ambiguity.

Performance Tier Breakdown

  • Entry Utility Pair (CX152FMH-5B & CX152FMH-6): Both models share a 52.4 × 49.5 mm bore and stroke, 9.7:1 compression ratio, 19 kg weight, and 477 × 395 × 280 mm package dimensions. The CX152FMH-6 (107 ml) provides a modest calibration increase over the CX152FMH-5B (106.7 ml), raising output from 4.7 kW / 6.7 N·m to 4.9 kW / 6.9 N·m for light commercial underbones and youth/utility quads.
  • Square-Bore Mid-Utility (CX152FMH-5B (130)): Featuring a 54 × 54 mm square bore-and-stroke (123.67 ml) and lower 9.0:1 compression ratio, this 23 kg unit peaks earlier at 6.3 kW @ 7,500 rpm and 8.8 N·m @ 5,500 rpm. The shorter 450 × 280 × 277 mm package and early torque peak suit heavier utility payloads and markets with varying fuel octane grades.
  • High-Output Trail & Pit-Bike Tier (CX1P56FMJ & CX1P60FMJ): The CX1P56FMJ (140 ml, 56 × 57 mm, YX140 reference) delivers balanced midrange pull (8.2 kW @ 8,000 rpm, 10.2 N·m @ 7,500 rpm) at 21 kg. Stepping up to the oversquare CX1P60FMJ (149.8 ml, 60 × 53 mm, W150-2 reference) lifts compression to 9.9:1 and extends peak power to 10.2 kW @ 9,500 rpm and 11.2 N·m @ 8,000 rpm inside the lineup's most compact envelope (435 × 285 × 235 mm, 22 kg).

2. Why CX1P60FMJ's Internal Cylinder-Head Oil Circuit Simplifies Installation

Higher-revving 150cc horizontal engines operating toward 9,500 rpm place heavy thermal demand on overhead valvetrain components. Conventional 150cc horizontal platforms often rely on external add-on oil radiators connected via external hoses and cylinder-head adapter plates. By contrast, CHIXIANG's CX1P60FMJ is air-cooled with a dedicated internal cylinder-head oil circuit, eliminating the need for an external oil radiator.

  • Fewer Oil-Leak Points: Removing external rubber or braided lines, banjo fittings, and crush washers eliminates common vibration-induced oil weeping and trail-impact hose ruptures.
  • Zero Frame-Bracket Fabrication: Assemblers do not need to weld radiator mounting tabs onto underbone or pit-bike steering heads, preserving clean front-frame geometry across the compact 435 × 285 × 235 mm footprint.
  • Faster Assembly & Lower BOM Cost: Eliminating the external cooler kit reduces per-unit hardware count, workshop installation time, and spare-part SKU complexity for aftermarket distributors.
  • Protected Off-Road Packaging: Without an exposed finned radiator sitting behind the front wheel, the engine avoids mud clogging and stone damage during off-road or rough rural operation (oil viscosity and fill specification are matched to destination climate).

3. Clutch Types, Optional 1+1 Reverse & Starter-Mount Verification Checklist

Before finalizing a wholesale horizontal engine order, engineers and purchasing managers should verify four chassis-interface variables to ensure direct frame alignment:

  1. Clutch Architecture Selection
    CX152FMH-5B, CX152FMH-6, and CX152FMH-5B (130) use an automatic wet clutch that eliminates the handlebar clutch lever—ideal for entry-level commuters, fleet delivery underbones, and recreational ATVs. For CX1P56FMJ and CX1P60FMJ, specify a manual hand-lever clutch for sport pit-bike and off-road throttle modulation, or a semi-auto clutch for utility vehicles prioritizing simplified shifting.
  2. Gearbox Pattern & Optional Built-In Reverse (1+1)
    All five models feature a 4-speed transmission as standard (verify shift sequence such as N-1-2-3-4 rotary vs. 1-N-2-3-4 international against your target market). For ATV and light utility quad applications, CHIXIANG offers an optional built-in 1+1 reverse gearbox on applicable horizontal models upon order specification.
  3. Upper vs. Lower Starter Motor Mount Confirmation
    Horizontal motorcycle frames vary significantly in downtube clearance, skid-plate height, and carburetor intake clearance. Confirm whether your target chassis requires an upper-mounted electric starter, a lower-mounted electric starter, or a kick-start-only crankcase before production scheduling.
  4. Output Sprocket, Electrical Harness & Commercial Supply Terms
    Verify front sprocket spline count and chain pitch, carburetor/intake manifold orientation, and stator wiring plug pinouts against your vehicle wiring harness. Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why do CX152FMH-6 and CX152FMH-5B (130) list actual displacements of 107 ml and 123.67 ml?

In horizontal engine export catalogs, 125 cc class (CX152FMH-6) and (130) (CX152FMH-5B (130)) serve as commercial series designations. CHIXIANG always discloses exact bore-and-stroke measurements—52.4 × 49.5 mm (107 ml) and 54 × 54 mm (123.67 ml)—so importers can verify customs declarations and replacement cylinder parts accurately.

2. Are CX1P56FMJ and CX1P60FMJ manufactured by YX?

No. Both models are manufactured by Chongqing Chixiang Motorcycle Manufacturing Co., Ltd. YX140 (CX1P56FMJ) and W150-2 (CX1P60FMJ) are cited solely as market-reference alignment so buyers can identify displacement class and vehicle application tier.

3. Does the 149.8 ml CX1P60FMJ require an external oil cooler?

No. The CX1P60FMJ is air-cooled and engineered with an internal cylinder-head oil circuit, meaning no external oil radiator, mounting bracket, or external oil hose routing is required during chassis installation.

4. Which CHIXIANG horizontal models offer manual clutches or a built-in 1+1 reverse gear?

CX1P56FMJ (140 ml) and CX1P60FMJ (149.8 ml) are available with manual or semi-auto clutches, while the CX152FMH series uses an automatic wet clutch. Optional built-in 1+1 reverse gearboxes are available on applicable ATV/utility horizontal models upon order specification.

5. What should importers confirm regarding starter mounts, MOQ, and warranty before ordering?

Verify upper vs. lower electric starter mounting clearance (or kick-start-only build) against your chassis frame before production. Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 complete engines (mixed models and spare parts supported), regular lead time is ~10 working days FOB Guangzhou, and all engines include a 60-day post-arrival warranty with free replacement parts shipped in the next container after serial number plus photo/video verification.
