Bajaj Boxer BM150 Replacement FAQ

Is CX-BJ-BM150-ENG an OEM Bajaj factory part or an aftermarket replacement?

CX-BJ-BM150-ENG is an independent aftermarket replacement engine manufactured by CHIXIANG MOTOR to 144.8 cc (56.0 × 58.8 mm) OEM-equivalent mounting and shaft dimensions. Bajaj® and OEM numbers are referenced solely for mechanical compatibility.

How do I choose between the 4-speed and 5-speed BM150 replacement versions?

Verify your fleet's existing transmission shift pattern and engine prefix (36PF / JA series reference) before ordering, and specify 4-speed or 5-speed international constant mesh plus single-spark or twin-spark ignition in your Proforma Invoice.

Which calibrated OEM reference numbers correspond to CHIXIANG BM150 core castings and kits?

Core references include 36PF0004 (LH Crankcase CX-BJ-BM150-LH), 36PF0005 (RH Crankcase CX-BJ-BM150-RH), 36PF0008 (Clutch Cover CX-BJ-BM150-CC), 36PF0002 (Cylinder & Piston Kit CX-BJ-BM150-CY), 36PF0009 (Clutch Plates in CX-BJ-BM150-TR), and JA541212 (Clutch Cover Gasket reference).

Can I combine BM150 complete engines with crankcases, cylinder kits, and Chinese CG/CB engines?

Yes. Standard wholesale MOQ is 50 units with mixed-container loading supported across BM150 engines, BM150 overhaul castings/kits, and CHIXIANG CG/CB/HW engine models.

What testing and warranty apply to BM150 replacement engines?

Every complete engine undergoes 100% pre-shipment hot and cold bench testing in Chongqing (~10 working days regular lead time, FOB Guangzhou) and carries a 60-day warranty after destination port arrival for verified manufacturing or assembly defects.
